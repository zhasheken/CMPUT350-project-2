Collision box coordinates
========================

CollisionComponent.collisionBox is stored relative to the entity's top-left
LocationComponent.loc. CollisionLateSystem snapshots the translated,
world-space box into CollisionComponent.previousCollisionBox before movement.
CollisionSystem compares that snapshot with current world-space boxes to
identify the side of a static obstacle that was hit and correct the entity's
location.

Star collision behavior
=======================

A star is a dynamic entity with CollisionComponent, LocationComponent and
MovementComponent, but without InputComponent. Stars do not receive gravity
from CollisionSystem; their velocity is supplied by other systems. Static
obstacle bounces use 0.7 restitution, with speeds below 1 pixel per second
settled to zero. Star-star collisions separate along the shallowest axis and
exchange velocity on that axis. Collection requires a ScoreComponent and a
sound registered as "star". The platformer registers sfx_gem.ogg for this.
Map loading must avoid placing a spawned star inside a static spawner box.

Input controls
==============

Horizontal input accelerates toward a capped run speed, with separate ground
and air acceleration; releasing both direction keys decelerates toward rest.
Jump and dash are press-triggered. Releasing jump while rising cuts upward
velocity. The dash cooldown is 60 InputSystem updates (one second at 60 FPS).
The wall-slide cue uses the available footstep_snow_001.ogg asset because no
dedicated wall-slide sound is present.
